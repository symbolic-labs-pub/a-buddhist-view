## [The Tri Dragulja (Trostruki Dragulj) — *Ti-ratana*](https://github.com/symbolic-labs-pub/a-buddhist-view/blob/languages/hr/master/more/01_core_teachings/the_three_jewels/README.md#the-three-jewels-triple-gem--ti-ratana)

[![the_three_jewels](the_three_jewels.png)](https://github.com/symbolic-labs-pub/a-buddhist-view/blob/languages/hr/master/more/01_core_teachings/the_three_jewels/README.md#the-three-jewels-triple-gem--ti-ratana)
In classical budistički učenje, **taking utočište** in the Tri Dragulja is not a pledge of belief but a **practical orientation of life**—a commitment to wake up to reality as it is, and to live accordingly. Utočište means *“that which protects from [patnja](../../02_from_ignorance_to_awakening/2_the_four_noble_truths/README.md#1-there-is-patnja--dukkha)”* (Pāli: *saraṇa*).

---

## 1) **Buddha** — the Awakened One, and buđenje itself


Historically, the Buddha refers to **Siddhārtha Gautama**, who realized liberation through direct insight into the nature of mind and reality.

Doctrinally, **Buddha** has three inseparable meanings:

1. **The historical Buddha** — proof that [buđenje](../../10_concepts/README.md#3-enlightenment-bodhi-buđenje) is humanly possible.
2. **Buddhahood** — the *state of buđenje*: complete freedom from ignorance (*avijjā*), craving (*taṇhā*), and patnja (*dukkha*).
3. [**Buddha-nature**](../../02_from_ignorance_to_awakening/6_buddha_nature/README.md#buddha-nature-tathāgatagarbha--explained-through-budistički-učenja) (emphasized in [Mahāyāna](../../05_yanas/README.md#limitation-from-mahāyāna-view)) — the *capacity for buđenje inherent in all beings*.

> Taking utočište in Buddha means trusting **buđenje over habit**, [mudrost](../the_noble_eightfold_path/README.md#1-mudrost-paññā) over impulse.

---

## 2) **Dharma** — the put and the law of reality


**Dharma (Dhamma)** operates on two levels simultaneously:

### a) **Učenja**

* The [Four Noble Truths](../../02_from_ignorance_to_awakening/2_the_four_noble_truths/README.md#the-four-noble-truths--as-the-buddha-meant-them)
* The [Noble Eightfold Put](../the_noble_eightfold_path/README.md#what-the-noble-eightfold-put-is-in-buddhism)
* [Ovisno Podrijetlo (*paṭicca-samuppāda*)](../../02_from_ignorance_to_awakening/3_dependent_origination/README.md#the-twelve-links-the-classic-formulation)
* [Impermanence (*anicca*)](../impermanence/README.md#2-impermanence-anicca-is-structural-not-accidental), [non-self (*anattā*)](../../02_from_ignorance_to_awakening/1_the_three_marks_of_existence/README.md#3-non-self-anattā), unsatisfactoriness (*dukkha*)

These are **diagnostic and therapeutic**, not metaphysical doctrines.

### b) **Reality itself**

Dharma also means *“the way things actually are”*:

* phenomena arise due to causes and conditions,
* nothing permanent or independent can be found,
* clinging inevitably produces patnja.

Crucially, the Buddha taught:

> **Dharma is to be realized, not believed.**

The učenja are compared to a **raft**—essential for crossing the river, but not to be carried once the crossing is complete.

---

## 3) **Sangha** — the living continuity of buđenje


Traditionally, **Sangha** means the community of ordained monks and nuns who preserve the učenja and discipline (*Vinaya*).

In broader and deeper senses:

1. **Practical Sangha** — all sincere practitioners who support one another in ethical living, [meditacija](../../08_lineage/README.md), and mudrost.
2. **Noble Sangha** — those who have directly realized stages of buđenje (stream-enterers and beyond).
3. **Relational Sangha** — the *field of awakened relationship* in which insight is sustained through dialogue, example, correction, and [suosjećanje](../../02_from_ignorance_to_awakening/7_compassion/README.md#suosjećanje-as-a-structural-principle-in-budistički-učenje).

The Buddha repeatedly emphasized that **isolation weakens praksa**, while wise companionship (*kalyāṇa-mittatā*) accelerates liberation.

---

## Functional Unity of the Tri Dragulja


The [Tri Dragulja](#the-three-jewels-triple-gem---ti-ratana) are not separate objekti but **one integrated system**:

| Jewel      | Function in Praksa                 |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Buddha** | The **possibility** and living proof |
| [**Dharma**](#2-dharma---the-put-and-the-law-of-reality) | The **method** and truth             |
| **Sangha** | The **supporting conditions**        |

Remove one, and praksa collapses:

* Without Buddha → no confidence in buđenje.
* Without Dharma → no direction.
* Without Sangha → no stability or correction.

---

## Utočište as an ongoing praksa


In early texts, utočište is taken **three times**, symbolizing:

* body, speech, and mind,
* past, present, and future,
* understanding, praksa, and realization.

But utočište is not a single event. It deepens as:

* confidence becomes insight,
* [ethics](../the_noble_eightfold_path/README.md#2-ethical-conduct-śīla) becomes spontaneous,
* effort becomes natural.

> Ultimately, taking utočište in the Tri Dragulja means **becoming them**:
> buđenje as Buddha, clarity as Dharma, and suosjećanje as Sangha.
