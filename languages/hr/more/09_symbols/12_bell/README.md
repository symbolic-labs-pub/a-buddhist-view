## [The Bell (Ghanta)](https://github.com/symbolic-labs-pub/a-buddhist-view/blob/languages/hr/master/more/09_symbols/12_bell/README.md#the-bell-ghanta)

[![bell](bell.png)](https://github.com/symbolic-labs-pub/a-buddhist-view/blob/languages/hr/master/more/09_symbols/12_bell/README.md#the-bell-ghanta)

---

The **bell (ghanta)** is one of the most important ritual objekti in **[Vajrayāna](../../05_yanas/README.md#4-vajrayāna-tantrayāna-mantrayāna---the-diamond-vehicle) Buddhism**, especially within Tibetan loze. It is never merely an instrument—it is a **učenje in physical form**.

---

### 1. Core Meaning: Mudrost and Emptiness

The bell represents **mudrost (prajñā)**, specifically the realization of **[emptiness (śūnyatā)](../../10_concepts/01_emptiness/README.md#emptiness-śūnyatā-in-vajrayāna-buddhism)**.

* **Emptiness** does not mean nothingness
* It means that all phenomena lack fixed, independent essence
* Everything arises **dependently**, fluidly, relationally

The bell’s sound embodies this insight:

* It **arises**, resonates, and **dissolves**
* Nothing solid remains
* [Awareness](../../10_concepts/README.md#2-awareness-rigpa-vijñāna-knowing) stays present without clinging

When the bell rings, it teaches [impermanence](../../01_core_teachings/impermanence/README.md#2-impermanence-anicca-is-structural-not-accidental) **directly**, without concepts.

---

### 2. Sound as Direct Insight

In budistički understanding, sound is not decoration—it is **instruction**.

The bell’s tone:

* Cuts through discursive razmišljanje
* Interrupts habitual mental narration
* Points attention toward **open awareness**

You do not analyze the sound.
You **listen**, and in listening, the mind relaxes into clarity.

This is why bells are used to:

* Begin and end [meditacija](../../08_lineage/README.md)
* Mark ritual transitions
* Reset attention during complex prakse

The sound teaches what words cannot.

---

### 3. Left Hand: Receptivity and Spaciousness

The bell is traditionally held in the **left hand**, which symbolizes:

* **Receptivity**
* **Openness**
* **Non-grasping awareness**

In contrast:

* The **[vajra (dorje)](../02_dorje/README.md#dorje-vajra--explained-according-to-budistički-učenja)** is held in the right hand (method, [suosjećanje](../../02_from_ignorance_to_awakening/7_compassion/README.md#suosjećanje-as-a-structural-principle-in-budistički-učenje), action)
* Together, bell and vajra express the **union of mudrost and method**

Without the bell:

* Suosjećanje becomes blind effort

Without the vajra:

* Mudrost becomes passive or detached

[Buđenje](../../10_concepts/README.md#3-enlightenment-bodhi-buđenje) requires **both**, inseparably.

---

### 4. Feminine Principle of Mudrost

In tantric symbolism, the bell represents the **feminine principle**:

* Mudrost as space
* Insight as otvorenost
* Awareness that allows all phenomena to arise and dissolve

This is not gendered in a biological sense.
It refers to **how mind knows**—by allowing, not grasping.

Thus, mudrost is not something you *do*.
It is something you **recognize**.

---

### 5. Why the Bell Is Never Used Casually

Traditionally:

* A bell is consecrated
* Used only in praksa contexts
* Treated as a **učitelj**, not a tool

Because every ring is a reminder:

> “All forms are empty.
> All sounds dissolve.
> Awareness remains.”

---

### 6. Inner Učenje (Most Important)

Ultimately, the bell points inward.

The true bell is:

* The moment awareness recognizes itself
* The silence after sound fades
* The clarity that remains without effort

If the bell is used correctly, it teaches this without explanation.
