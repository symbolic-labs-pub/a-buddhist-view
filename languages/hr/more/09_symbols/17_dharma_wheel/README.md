---

The **[Dharma](../../01_core_teachings/the_three_jewels/README.md#2-dharma--the-put-and-the-law-of-reality) Wheel** is the primary symbol of Buddhism because it encodes *how [buđenje](../../10_concepts/README.md#3-enlightenment-bodhi-buđenje) functions in the world*. It is not decorative; it is **operational doctrine**.

---

### 1. **Učenje in Motion**

When the Buddha gave his first sermon at Sarnath, tradition calls this **“turning the wheel of Dharma.”**
This expresses a core insight:

* Truth is not static doctrine
* Dharma **moves**, **adapts**, and **acts**
* Učenje must be *applied*, not merely understood

A wheel only fulfills its purpose **while turning**. Likewise, Dharma exists only when practiced—through conduct, [meditacija](../../08_lineage/README.md), and insight.

---

### 2. **Ethical Orientation (Navigation, not Rules)**

The wheel functions like a **compass**, not a commandment tablet.

* The hub = **stable [awareness](../../10_concepts/README.md#2-awareness-rigpa-vijñāna-knowing)**
* The rim = **lived experience**
* The spokes = **guiding principles**

[Ethics](../../01_core_teachings/the_noble_eightfold_path/README.md#2-ethical-conduct-śīla) in Buddhism are *directional*: they orient the mind away from [patnja](../../02_from_ignorance_to_awakening/2_the_four_noble_truths/README.md#1-there-is-patnja--dukkha) and toward clarity.
Right and wrong are not moral absolutes, but **causal intelligences**—what leads to liberation vs. entanglement.

---

### 3. **Eightfold Structure**

The **eight spokes** represent the [**Noble Eightfold Put**](../../01_core_teachings/the_noble_eightfold_path/README.md#what-the-noble-eightfold-put-is-in-buddhism), divided into three integrated domains:

[**Mudrost**](../../01_core_teachings/the_noble_eightfold_path/README.md#1-mudrost-paññā)

* Right View
* Right Intention

**Ethical Conduct**

* Right Speech
* Right Action
* Right Livelihood

**Mental Discipline**

* Right Effort
* Right [Mindfulness](../../01_core_teachings/the_noble_eightfold_path/README.md#7-right-mindfulness-sammā-sati)
* Right [Concentration](../../01_core_teachings/the_noble_eightfold_path/README.md#8-right-concentration-sammā-samādhi)

Crucially:

* No spoke dominates
* If one breaks, the wheel collapses
* Buđenje requires **structural balance**

---

### 4. **The Hub: Non-Dual Stability**

The unmoving center of the wheel symbolizes **[emptiness](../../10_concepts/01_emptiness/README.md#emptiness-śūnyatā-in-vajrayāna-buddhism) and non-clinging**.

* Thoughts arise and pass (rim)
* Activity continues (spokes)
* Awareness remains still (hub)

This teaches a subtle but profound point:

> Liberation is not stopping movement — it is **not being thrown by it**.

---

### 5. **Why the Wheel, Not a Sword or Book**

Other traditions emphasize:

* Law (tablets)
* Salvation (cross)
* Power (sword)

Buddhism emphasizes **process**.

The wheel conveys:

* Continuity over revelation
* Training over belief
* Causality over authority

---

### 6. **Meditative Insight**

In praksa, the Dharma Wheel reminds practitioners to ask:

* Is my life *turning* in alignment with insight?
* Are my actions reinforcing patnja or clarity?
* Is awareness centered, or dragged by the rim?

The wheel does not judge — it **reveals trajectory**.

---

### Core Učenje Summary

> The Dharma Wheel teaches that buđenje is not a state to possess,
> but a **direction to sustain**.

When the wheel turns smoothly, patnja decreases.
When it warps, friction appears.
Praksa is the art of **keeping the wheel true**.
