# The Bodhisattva Put


## Buđenje Is Not Private


### A Učenje on Buđenje-in-Relation

---

## 1. The Core Reversal


Ordinary razmišljanje assumes:

> *I awaken, then I help others.*

The **bodhisattva put reverses this**:

> *Buđenje occurs only in relation to others.*

This is not moral idealism.
It is a **diagnosis of how mind actually functions**.

As long as buđenje is imagined as *personal attainment*, subtle self-clinging remains.
Where self-clinging remains, buđenje is incomplete.

---

## 2. Why Enlightenment Cannot Be Private


From the budistički view:

* [Patnja](../../02_from_ignorance_to_awakening/2_the_four_noble_truths/README.md#1-there-is-patnja--dukkha) arises through **misperception of separation**
* Liberation arises through **viđenje relationality clearly**

If [buđenje](../../10_concepts/README.md#3-enlightenment-bodhi-buđenje) were private, it would reinforce the very illusion it claims to end.

Thus, the bodhisattva vow is not altruism.
It is **ontological correctness**.

To awaken while excluding others would be like trying to breathe while denying air.

---

## 3. Mudrost and Suosjećanje: Not Two Things


In early praksa, mudrost and suosjećanje appear distinct:

* **Mudrost** sees [impermanence](../../01_core_teachings/impermanence/README.md#2-impermanence-anicca-is-structural-not-accidental), [emptiness](../../10_concepts/01_emptiness/README.md#emptiness-śūnyatā-in-vajrayāna-buddhism), [non-self](../../02_from_ignorance_to_awakening/1_the_three_marks_of_existence/README.md#3-non-self-anattā)
* **Suosjećanje** responds to patnja

On the bodhisattva put, they are understood as **two expressions of one insight**:

* Mudrost without suosjećanje becomes cold detachment
* Suosjećanje without mudrost becomes exhaustion

When emptiness is truly seen, separation collapses.
When separation collapses, care becomes automatic.

Thus, suosjećanje is **emptiness in action**.

---

## 4. The Bodhisattva Vow as Structural Alignment


The vow is often misunderstood as a promise to *save all beings*.

This is impossible if taken literally — and Buddhism does not teach impossibilities.

The vow actually does this:

* It **removes self-exemption**
* It aligns perception with interdependence
* It prevents buđenje from crystallizing into identitet

The vow reshapes **how perception functions**, not what outcomes must occur.

It is a **direction**, not a finish line.

---

## 5. Buđenje in Motion


A Buddha represents **complete realization at rest**.
A bodhisattva represents **realization in movement**.

This distinction matters.

Life is dynamic:

* Conditions shift
* Patnja changes form
* Responses must adapt

The bodhisattva embodies buđenje that:

* Thinks
* Speaks
* Acts
* Adjusts

Not perfectly — but responsively.

Buđenje is no longer a state.
It is a **way of functioning**.

---

## 6. The Ethical Consequence


Because buđenje is relational:

* [Ethics](../../01_core_teachings/the_noble_eightfold_path/README.md#2-ethical-conduct-śīla) are not rules
* They are expressions of clarity

A bodhisattva does not ask:

> *Is this allowed?*

But:

> *Does this reduce confusion or increase it?*

Ethical conduct emerges naturally when:

* Self-centered reference weakens
* Cause and effect are seen clearly

This is why morality precedes, supports, and expresses realization.

---

## 7. The Silent Criterion


A simple test reveals bodhisattva alignment:

> Does this thought, word, or action
> make buđenje more available — or less — for others?

If it contracts the field, it is not yet complete.
If it opens the field, it accords with the put.

This test applies equally to:

* Speech
* Work
* Politics
* Technology
* Učenje
* Silence

The bodhisattva put does not retreat from the world.
It **inhabits it intelligently**.

---

## 8. Final Pointing


The bodhisattva is not a saint, savior, or hero.

A bodhisattva is someone who has understood one thing clearly:

> There is no liberation that excludes.

Therefore:

* Buđenje continues
* Praksa continues
* Responsiveness continues

Not because enlightenment is lacking —
but because **reality is alive**.

---

### Closing Line (Traditional in Spirit)

> May mudrost clarify perception.
> May suosjećanje guide response.
> May buđenje move freely through the world.

This is the **bodhisattva učenje**.

## Bodhisattva Meditacija

### *Buđenje-in-Relation Praksa*

This is not a visualization of a deity.
It is a **training in orientation**.

> ⚠️ **Note on scope**
> What follows is a **non-empowerment contemplative form** (a *praksa of meaning*).
> It does **not** replace loza transmission (*wang, lung, tri*).
> Its function is **stabilization, aspiration, and causal alignment**, not tantric authorization.

---

## 1. Preparation — Establish the Ground

Sit comfortably. Spine upright, body relaxed.
Let the breath settle without controlling it.

Bring to mind:

> *Buđenje is not for me alone.*

Do not make this emotional.
Let it land as a **structural fact**.

---

## 2. Opening Contemplation — The Bodhisattva Vow

Silently reflect:

* Every being seeks relief from patnja
* No being awakens in isolation
* Mudrost without suosjećanje fractures
* Suosjećanje without mudrost exhausts

Let these reflections **re-pattern motivation**, not mood.

Then gently form the intention:

> *May this praksa benefit all beings, directly or indirectly.*

This intention is the **axis** of the meditacija.

---

## 3. Core Praksa — Co-Arising of Mudrost and Suosjećanje

### Phase A: Mudrost (Seeing Clearly)

Rest awareness on experience as it is:

* Sensations arise
* Thoughts arise
* Emotions arise

Notice:

* None are solid
* None remain
* None belong to a fixed “self”

Do not analyze.
Just **see arising and dissolving**.

This is **prajñā** — clarity without commentary.

---

### Phase B: Suosjećanje (Responding Without Grasping)

Now widen awareness to include others:

* People you know
* People you don’t know
* Beings struggling in obvious and invisible ways

Do not imagine stories.
Simply recognize:

> *This patnja feels real to them.*

Allow a **non-sentimental warmth** to emerge.

This is **karuṇā** — responsiveness without self-reference.

---

### Phase C: Integration — Buđenje in Motion

Now drop the distinction.

Let clarity and care function **together**:

* Seeing patnja clearly
* Responding without clinging
* Acting without needing credit

Rest here.

This is the **bodhisattva mode**:

> Awareness that moves.

---

## 4. Working with Obstacles

If pride arises:

* Recognize it as fixation
* Let it dissolve

If fatigue arises:

* Recognize suosjećanje without mudrost
* Return to clarity

If emptiness feels cold:

* Let suosjećanje warm it

If suosjećanje feels heavy:

* Let mudrost lighten it

Obstacles are **diagnostics**, not failures.

---

## 5. Dedication — Sealing the Praksa

Conclude with dedication:

> *Whatever clarity or benefit arose here
> may it become a cause for the buđenje of all beings.*

Do not dramatize this.
Dedication **stabilizes causality**.

---

## 6. Off-Cushion Continuation — The Real Praksa

The bodhisattva vow lives **outside meditacija**.

Train in three daily recognitions:

1. **Before speaking**
   Ask: *Does this reduce or increase confusion?*

2. **Before acting**
   Ask: *Does this tighten or loosen patnja?*

3. **When reacting**
   Ask: *What would clarity plus care look like here?*

No heroics.
No perfectionism.

Just **consistent orientation**.

---

## Key Insight to Carry

> A bodhisattva is not someone who saves the world.
> A bodhisattva is someone who **stops excluding others from buđenje**.
