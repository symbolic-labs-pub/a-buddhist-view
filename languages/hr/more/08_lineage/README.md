## [White Tārā (Sitatārā) – The Mother of Longevity & Suosjećanje](01_white_tara/README.md)

| [![white_tara_small](01_white_tara/white_tara_small.png)](01_white_tara/README.md#white-tārā--the-učenje-of-sustaining-conditions) | 

**White [Tara](#green-tārā--syamatārā---swift-suosjećanje-in-action)** embodies **[suosjećanje](#green-tārā--syamatārā---swift-suosjećanje-in-action) izraženo kao liječenje i dugi život**.

U Kagyü tradiciji:

* She is invoked za **dugovječnost**, **zdravlje**, i **stabilnost of praksa**
* Njezino **sedam očiju** simbolizira svevidnu [svjesnost](../10_concepts/README.md#2-awareness-rigpa-vijñāna-knowing)
* Praksa emphasizes **gentle presence, continuity, and nurturing mudrost**

Bijela Tārā nije apstraktna milosrđa—ona je **skrb koja održava uvjete za [buđenje](../10_concepts/README.md#3-enlightenment-bodhi-buđenje)**.

## [Green Tārā (Syamatārā) – Swift Suosjećanje in Action](02_green_tara/README.md)

[![green_tara_small](02_green_tara/green_tara_small.png)](02_green_tara/README.md#a-kagyü-učenje-on-green-tārā-suosjećanje-that-does-not-hesitate)

**Zelena Tara** predstavlja **trenutno, bezstrašno suosjećajno djelovanje**.

U Kagyü razumijevanju:

* Ona odgovara **trenutno** na strah i prepreke
* Jedna noga proširena = **spremnost za djelovanje**
* Posebno prakticirana u vremenima opasnosti, anksioznosti ili prijelaza

Zelena Tārā trenira praktikanta da **djeluje iz jasnoće, ne iz oklijevanja**.

Ključne točke:

* Wrathful appearance = **fierce suosjećanje**
* Destroys **ego-clinging, obstacles, and spiritual corruption**
* Protector **of praksa**, not of comfort

Mahākāla is **the energy that prevents regression**.

## [Thousand-Armed Avalokiteśvara (Chenrezig) – Infinite Suosjećanje](04_avalokitesvara/README.md)

[![avalokitesvara_small](04_avalokitesvara/avalokitesvara_small.png)](04_avalokitesvara/README.md#the-učenje-of-infinite-responsiveness)

[**Avalokiteśvara**](04_avalokitesvara/README.md#practical-integration-off-cushion), in his thousand-armed form, embodies **unlimited compassionate activity**.

In the Kagyü put:

* Central to **mind training (lojong)**
* Suosjećanje is not emotion, but **structural orientation**
* The many arms represent **skillful means applied everywhere**

This praksa **softens self-fixation while strengthening resolve**.

## [Guru Rinpoche / Padmasambhava – The Tantric Master](05_padmasambhava/README.md)

[![padmasambhava_small](05_padmasambhava/padmasambhava_small.png)](05_padmasambhava/README.md#budistički-učenje-buđenje-as-skillful-adaptation)

**Guru Rinpoche**, also known as **Padmasambhava**, is the **embodiment of enlightened method**.

Though central to the Nyingma school, in Kagyü:

* He represents **fearless tantric mastery**
* Integrates **wrathful and peaceful means**
* Invoked in advanced [Vajrayāna](../05_yanas/README.md#4-vajrayāna-tantrayāna-mantrayāna---the-diamond-vehicle) contexts

Guru Rinpoche shows that **buđenje adapts to conditions**.

[![medicine_buddha_small](06_medicine_buddha/medicine_buddha_small.png)](06_medicine_buddha/README.md#a-budistički-učenje)

[**Medicine Buddha**](06_medicine_buddha/README.md#2-the-medicine-buddha’s-reorientation) manifests **[mudrost](../01_core_teachings/the_noble_eightfold_path/README.md#1-mudrost-paññā) as healing clarity**.

In Kagyü usage:

* Healing applies to **mind, karma, and conditions**

* Praksa refines awareness of **cause and effect**

Healing here means **viđenje clearly what must be changed**.

## [Bodhisattva – The Vow of Buđenje-for-All](08_bodhisattva/README.md)

[![bodhisattva_small](08_bodhisattva/bodhisattva_small.png)](08_bodhisattva/README.md#buđenje-is-not-private)

A [**Bodhisattva**](08_bodhisattva/README.md#4-the-bodhisattva-vow-as-structural-alignment) is not a deity, but a **mode of being**.

In Kagyü view:

* Enlightenment je nepotpuna without others
* Suosjećanje and mudrost **co-arise**
* The vow shapes **every action and perception**

A bodhisattva is **buđenje in motion**.

## Strukturni Uvid (Kagyü Lens)

These figures are **not worship objekti**, but **functional archetypes**:

* **Tārās** → compassionate responsiveness
* **Protectors** → structural integrity of praksa
* **Bodhisattvas** → ethical direction
* **Gurus** → transmission of realization
* **Buddhas** → stabilized awakened states

Together they form a **praksa ecosystem**, not a pantheon.

## [Milarepa — The Archetype of Radical Transformation](09_milarepa/README.md)

[![milarepa_small](09_milarepa/milarepa_small.png)](09_milarepa/README.md#a-budistički-učenje-milarepa-and-the-law-of-irreversible-transformation)

[**Milarepa**](09_milarepa/README.md#a-budistički-učenje-milarepa-and-the-law-of-irreversible-transformation) is the **embodied proof** that buđenje is possible **within a single lifetime**, regardless of one's past.

**Kagyü meaning**

* Formerly burdened by grave karma, he attained full realization through **austere meditacija**
* Lived as a **mountain yogi**, practicing in isolation
* Known for spontaneous **songs of realization (dohas)**

**Praksa function**

* Represents **irreversible transformation**
* Teaches that **renunciation + devotion + perseverance** dissolve even the heaviest obscurations
* Central inspirational figure for **retreat culture** in the Kagyü loza

Milarepa shows that **nothing disqualifies buđenje except refusal to praksa**.

## [Marpa the Translator — Holder of Authentic Transmission](10_marpa/README.md)

[![marpa_small](10_marpa/marpa_small.png)](10_marpa/README.md#a-budistički-učenje-marpa-the-translator-the-dharma-that-refuses-to-be-softened)

[**Marpa**](10_marpa/README.md#a-budistički-učenje-marpa-the-translator--the-dharma-that-refuses-to-be-softened), known as *Marpa Lotsāwa*, is the **architect of Kagyü transmission**.

**Kagyü meaning**

* Travelled repeatedly to India to receive **tantric učenja directly**
* Translated texts with extreme precision
* Passed the loza intact to Milarepa

**Praksa function**

* Embodies **loza fidelity**
* Demonstrates that suosjećanje may appear **strict or demanding**
* Ensures that realization is **not diluted by comfort or sentimentality**

Marpa represents **structural integrity of buđenje**—truth transmitted without distortion.

## [Vajradhara — The Source of Mahāmudrā](11_vajradhara/README.md)

[![vajradhara_small](11_vajradhara/vajradhara_small.png)](11_vajradhara/README.md#vajradhara-and-the-učenje-of-recognition)

[**Vajradhara**](11_vajradhara/README.md#vajradhara-and-the-učenje-of-recognition) is the **primordial Buddha** in the Kagyü tradition.

**Kagyü meaning**

* Source of **[Mahāmudrā](../04_kayas/mahamudra_and_dzogcsen/README.md#mahāmudrā-nature-of-mind-སེམས་ཀྱི་གནས་ལུགས་) realization**
* Not historical, but **timeless awareness itself**
* Depicted holding [vajra](../09_symbols/02_dorje/README.md#dorje-vajra--explained-according-to-budistički-učenja) and bell—**method and mudrost unified**

**Praksa function**

* Represents the **ground state of mind**
* Not something to attain, but **to recognize**
* Mahāmudrā instructions point directly to Vajradhara-nature within one’s own awareness

Vajradhara is **buđenje before concepts arise**.

## [Black Six-Armed Mahākāla — Esoteric Dharma Protector](12_six_armed_mahakala/README.md)

[![six_armed_mahakala_small](12_six_armed_mahakala/six_armed_mahakala_small.png)](12_six_armed_mahakala/README.md#mahākāla-as-the-discipline-of-buđenje)

**Mahākāla** (Six-Armed, Black form) is the **most esoteric protector** of the Kagyü loza.

**Kagyü meaning**

* Emanation of awakened suosjećanje in **wrathful form**
* Six arms symbolize **mastery over the six realms**
* Acts swiftly and uncompromisingly

**Praksa function**

* Protects **advanced Vajrayāna praksa**
* Removes **inner obstacles** (ego, laziness, corruption)
* Invoked only with proper empowerment and discipline

This Mahākāla does not protect comfort—**he protects realization**.

## Strukturni Uvid (Kagyü Perspective)

Together, these four form a **complete vertical axis of the Kagyü put**:

* **Vajradhara** → ultimate nature of mind
* **Marpa** → flawless transmission
* **Milarepa** → total transformation through praksa
* **Six-Armed Mahākāla** → uncompromising protection of the put

They are not symbolic decorations; they are **functional necessities** in a loza built on **direktnu realizaciju**.
