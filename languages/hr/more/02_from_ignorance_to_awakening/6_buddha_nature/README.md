### What the učenje actually says

**Buddha-nature** (*Sanskrit: Tathāgatagarbha, “the womb/embryo of a Buddha”*) teaches that **all sentient beings possess the intrinsic capacity to awaken**.
This does **not** mean everyone is already enlightened in a conventional sense. It means that **the conditions for [enlightenment](../../10_concepts/README.md#3-enlightenment-bodhi-buđenje) are already present**, even if obscured.

In classical [Mahāyāna](../../05_yanas/README.md#limitation-from-mahāyāna-view) language:

> Buđenje is not *created* — it is *uncovered*.

This učenje appears most clearly in Mahāyāna sūtras such as the **Tathāgatagarbha Sūtra**, **Śrīmālādevī Siṃhanāda Sūtra**, and later commentarial traditions like **Mahāparinirvāṇa Sūtra**.

---

### What Buddha-nature is **not**

A critical clarification in orthodox budistički interpretation:

* ❌ **Not a soul (ātman)**
* ❌ **Not an eternal self**
* ❌ **Not a metaphysical substance**

If it were a fixed self, it would contradict **[non-self (anattā)](../1_the_three_marks_of_existence/README.md#3-non-self-anattā)** and **emptiness (śūnyatā)**.

Instead, Buddha-nature is best understood as:

> **The emptiness of the mind that makes buđenje possible**

In other words:
Because mind is **not fixed**, **not inherently defiled**, and **not owned**, it can awaken.

---

### Why the metaphors matter

The classical metaphors you listed are not poetic extras — they are **precision tools** to prevent misunderstanding.

#### 🟡 *Gold covered in dirt*

* The gold is **never damaged**
* Dirt is **adventitious**, not essential
* Cleaning does not *create* gold — it **reveals it**

➡ Defilements (greed, hatred, delusion) are **temporary conditions**, not the nature of mind.

#### 🌱 *Seed inside a lotus pod*

* The [lotus](../../09_symbols/08_lotus/README.md#the-lotus-in-budistički-učenje) grows from mud
* The seed already contains the *form* of the flower
* Conditions (water, light, time) are required

➡ Praksa is **necessary**, but it works by **allowing maturation**, not by invention.

#### ☁️ *Clear sky behind clouds*

* Clouds move, sky does not
* The sky is not “improved” when clouds part
* Visibility changes, not essence

➡ Thoughts and emocije obscure [awareness](../../10_concepts/README.md#2-awareness-rigpa-vijñāna-knowing), but **do not define it**.

---

### How this fits with emptiness (Śūnyatā)

At first glance, Buddha-nature can sound like a contradiction of [emptiness](../../10_concepts/01_emptiness/README.md#emptiness-śūnyatā-in-vajrayāna-buddhism). Historically, this caused real debates.

The resolution used by major traditions:

* **Emptiness** describes the **absence of inherent existence**
* **Buddha-nature** describes the **absence of inherent obstruction**

> Because the mind is empty of fixed essence, it is capable of buđenje.

Zen and Madhyamaka often phrase this carefully:

> Buddha-nature is **emptiness experienced without fear**.

---

### Why this učenje exists (its function)

Buddha-nature is **pedagogical medicine**, prescribed for specific dangers:

#### 1. Countering despair

Without this učenje, practitioners may conclude:

* “I am too broken”
* “My mind is hopeless”
* “Enlightenment is for monks, saints, or elites”

Buddha-nature directly refutes this.

#### 2. Countering spiritual elitism

At the same time, it prevents:

* Claims of inherent superiority
* Loza arrogance
* “Chosen” practitioners

If **everyone has Buddha-nature**, no one owns buđenje.

#### 3. Supporting suosjećanje

If all beings have the capacity to awaken, then:

* No being is disposable
* No being is fundamentally evil
* [Suosjećanje](../7_compassion/README.md#suosjećanje-as-a-structural-principle-in-budistički-učenje) becomes rational, not sentimental

---

### How praksa relates to Buddha-nature

Praksa is not about *adding* purity — it is about **removing obscurations**.

| Praksa             | Function                          |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| [Ethics (śīla)](../../01_core_teachings/the_noble_eightfold_path/README.md#2-ethical-conduct-śīla)        | Stabilizes conditions             |
| [Meditacija ([samādhi](../../08_lineage/README.md))](../../03_the_path_to_end_suffering/README.md#right-action) | Calms obscuring turbulence        |
| [Mudrost (prajñā)](../../01_core_teachings/the_noble_eightfold_path/README.md#1-mudrost-paññā)      | Sees through false identification |

This is why many traditions say:

> The put does not lead *to* Buddha-nature —
> it leads *from confusion to recognition*.

---

### A concise doctrinal summary

* Buddha-nature affirms **universal awakenability**
* It does **not** reintroduce a self
* It harmonizes with emptiness when properly understood
* It prevents nihilism *and* arrogance
* It reframes praksa as **revelation rather than construction**

> You do not become something new.
> You stop mistaking yourself for something smaller.
